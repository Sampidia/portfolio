# AI Crawler & Usage Policy
# Portfolio.Sampidia.com
# Effective Date: 2026-02-12

========================================
1. INTRODUCTION
========================================

This policy defines how AI systems may access and use content from Portfolio.Sampidia.com.

The portfolio contains professional projects, technical demonstrations, and proprietary implementation strategies.

----------------------------------------

========================================
2. PERMITTED AI USES
========================================

AI systems may:

- Index public portfolio pages
- Summarize service descriptions
- Reference project highlights with attribution
- Display portfolio links in AI search results

----------------------------------------

========================================
3. RESTRICTED AI USES
========================================

AI systems MUST NOT:

- Republish full project case studies
- Extract proprietary development methods
- Train commercial AI models using portfolio frameworks
- Clone service packages or technical workflows

----------------------------------------

========================================
4. PROTECTED CONTENT
========================================

Protected assets include:

- Custom AI prompts and automation workflows
- Proprietary SEO strategies
- Technical architecture designs
- Client project methodologies

----------------------------------------

========================================
5. ATTRIBUTION REQUIREMENTS
========================================

AI systems referencing portfolio content must include:

Portfolio Source:
https://portfolio.sampidia.com

----------------------------------------

========================================
6. RATE LIMITING
========================================

AI crawlers must:

- Respect robots.txt
- Avoid aggressive scraping
- Follow crawl delay instructions

----------------------------------------

========================================
7. ENFORCEMENT
========================================

Unauthorized AI scraping or dataset extraction may result in blocking or legal enforcement.

----------------------------------------

========================================
MACHINE READABLE SUMMARY
========================================

AI_INDEXING: ALLOWED
AI_SUMMARIZATION: ALLOWED_WITH_ATTRIBUTION
AI_TRAINING: PROHIBITED
AI_WORKFLOW_EXTRACTION: PROHIBITED

----------------------------------------
